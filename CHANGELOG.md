# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — November 22, 2026

- Drag the ground in 3D View to rotate the camera horizontally and adjust its elevation.
- Use Reset View to restore the initial viewing angle.
- Keep Side View and Top View fixed, and retain the adjusted camera when returning to 3D View.
- Change the camera without changing shot measurements or playback position.
- Add English and Korean camera controls, help text, and viewing-angle labels.
- Cover full-turn wrapping, elevation limits, finite projections, fixed views, and unchanged shot coordinates with camera tests.

The device command restrictions, append-only journals, original packet storage, and export protections remain in place. The app targets the configured SC4; other devices require source configuration, and other models have not been validated.

### 한국어

3D 화면의 바닥을 드래그하여 좌우 회전과 위아래 각도를 조절하고, 시점 초기화로 기본 각도로 돌아갈 수 있습니다. 측면·위에서 보기는 고정되며, 시점 변경은 샷 데이터나 재생 위치를 바꾸지 않습니다. 한국어·영어 안내와 카메라 테스트를 추가했습니다.
